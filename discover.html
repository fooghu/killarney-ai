Nature & Landscape

Discover Killarney

26,000 acres of National Park, three ancient lakes, glacial valleys and Ireland's highest mountains.

Home › Discover

Nature on a scale that stops you in your tracks

Killarney National Park was the first area in Ireland to receive UNESCO Biosphere Reserve status. Ancient oak woodland, three glacial lakes, red deer, white-tailed eagles and the MacGillycuddy's Reeks — Ireland's highest mountain range — all within a few miles of the town centre.
