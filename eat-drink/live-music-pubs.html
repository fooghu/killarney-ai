Live Music & Pub Sessions

Traditional sessions, live bands and the best pub music in Killarney — any night of the week.

Home›Eat & Drink›Live Music & Pub Sessions

Killarney has live music every night of the week in summer — and the range is wider than many visitors expect. Traditional Irish sessions sit alongside singer-songwriters, cover bands and the occasional original act. The key is knowing which pubs are running genuine traditional music and which are running something designed to look like it. This guide focuses on the real thing.

What is a traditional session?

A session — seisiún in Irish — is an informal gathering of musicians playing traditional Irish music together in a pub. It is not a performance in the formal sense — musicians arrive, join in, play tunes they know, and the standard rises and falls with who turns up. The audience is welcome to listen but the music is not being played for them; it is being played because the musicians want to play it. The best sessions have this quality of self-sufficiency — they would happen with or without the audience.

A genuine traditional session is one of the great Irish social institutions — music not performed but shared, in a pub that smells of turf smoke and good beer.

Where to find sessions in Killarney

O'Connor's

Traditional sessions · Most nights in summer · Authentic

One of the most reliable venues for genuine traditional sessions in Killarney — the musicians who play here are locals and regulars, and the standard is consistently high. Sessions typically start around 9pm.

O'Donoghue's

Serious traditional music · Less tourist-oriented

A pub with a strong reputation among musicians — sessions here attract players who take the tradition seriously. Less flashy than some of the Main Street options but more authentic.

The Laurels

Nightly music · Lively · Mixed programme

Live music every night in summer — a mix of traditional sessions, singer-songwriters and covers. The atmosphere is excellent and it runs late. More tourist-oriented than O'Connor's but still good fun.

Danny Mann Inn

Traditional · Nightly in season · Welcoming

A well-established music pub with nightly traditional sessions in summer — consistently popular and a reliable option for visitors looking for their first Kerry session experience.

Tips for session-goers

Sessions typically start late — 9pm to 9:30pm is standard in Killarney. Arriving early gives you a good seat and time for a pint before the music begins. It is customary to listen attentively during tunes rather than talking over them — the musicians are not background noise. Buying a musician a drink is a welcome gesture and a genuine part of session culture. Requests are generally not made or expected.

Live bands and other music

Beyond traditional sessions, Killarney has a busy live music calendar in summer — singer-songwriters in the hotel bars, cover bands in the larger pubs, and occasional original acts. The Killarney Junction Festival in October brings a concentrated programme of live music across multiple venues. Check local listings on arrival for what's on during your visit.
