About killarney.ai

An independent guide to Killarney

Written in Killarney. Checked at the source. No paid rankings — ever.

Home›About

killarney.ai is a guide to Killarney — the town, the National Park, and the corner of Kerry around them. It's for people planning a trip, people already here wondering what to do this afternoon, and anyone from Killarney now living far away who likes to keep an eye on home.

It isn't run by a tourism board, a business association or a booking site. That leaves one simple question to ask about any place: is it worth your time?

How we work

What you can rely on

01

No paid rankings

Places are in the guides because they're good, not because they paid. If we ever feature a business commercially, it's labelled as sponsored — and it never changes where anyone ranks.

02

Real places only

Every business, event and date on this site is real. No placeholder listings, no filler venues, no reviews nobody wrote.

03

Checked at the source

Wherever we can, facts come from the people who'd know — the National Park, the venues, the organisers — not from travel sites copying each other.

04

Kept current

Killarney changes with the seasons, and so does this guide. Event dates are confirmed every year, and when something closes or moves, the page changes too.

The name

Why “.ai”

The name is deliberate. AI is a big part of how this guide gets made.

Behind the scenes, AI tools do the heavy lifting: researching, organising, and keeping dozens of guides consistent and up to date. That's work that used to need a whole editorial team. But AI doesn't get the final say. It can't tell you which session is worth staying for on a wet Tuesday in March, or when to set off for the Gap of Dunloe to beat the crowds. That takes someone who lives here.

Yes, AI is used to build this site. But every piece of information is verified by someone local — someone who knows what Killarney is really like on the ground, not just what the internet says about it.

And if something you read or hear here was produced directly by AI, we'll tell you.

For local businesses

You can't buy your way in. You can stand out.

If you run a business in or around Killarney, here's how it works. You get into these guides by being good at what you do — that's free, and it isn't for sale.

If you'd like more visibility, sponsorship and feature opportunities are available — always labelled, and kept completely separate from the guides. That separation is the whole reason the guides are worth reading — for visitors, and for you.

Talk to us

Who's behind it

killarney.ai is made by Bart.

Bart works on the concierge desk at one of Killarney's top hotels, so his days are spent helping visitors get the most out of this place. He's a community enthusiast with a real love for the town, its people and the nature all around it — and this site is his way of giving something back.

“Killarney has a magical charm over me. I want to help this community, and the nature that makes it so special — and this is my way of doing it.”

— Bart

The guides are where the real work is, so have a look around — and if they help, come back.
