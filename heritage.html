Celtic & Ancient History

Heritage

4,500 years of human history — where ancient copper mines, island monasteries and standing stones meet the wild Kerry landscape.

Home › Heritage

Standing in a monastery overlooking an ancient glacial lake

Killarney's heritage goes far deeper than Victorian mansions and jaunting cars. The lakes and mountains that define the landscape have been sacred, inhabited and worked by humans for millennia — from Bronze Age miners to early Christian monks to Gaelic chieftains.

Early Christian Sites

⛪

7th Century · Island Monastery

Innisfallen Island

One of Ireland's most important early Christian sites — a monastery founded in the 7th century where the Annals of Innisfallen were written over 400 years.

15th Century · Franciscan Friary

Muckross Abbey

Remarkably well-preserved Franciscan friary founded in 1448 — its ancient yew tree in the cloister is said to be as old as the abbey itself.

Round Tower · Romanesque Church

Aghadoe

A hilltop site with the remains of a round tower and Romanesque church — overlooking the full sweep of the Killarney lakes and mountains.

Prehistoric & Celtic

⛏️

4,500 Years Old · Bronze Age

Ross Island Copper Mines

Among the oldest copper mines in Europe — Bronze Age miners extracted copper here over 4,500 years ago, helping to kickstart the Bronze Age in Ireland and Britain.

Read more →
🪨

Bronze Age · Stone Circle

Kenmare Stone Circle

One of the largest stone circles in southwest Ireland — fifteen standing stones arranged in a Bronze Age ceremonial ring near the town of Kenmare.

Read more →
🌀

Celtic Ireland

Ringforts & Ogham Stones

Kerry has one of the highest concentrations of ringforts in Ireland — circular Celtic farmsteads dotting the landscape alongside rare Ogham-inscribed standing stones.

Read more →

Living Heritage

🐐

Ancient Celtic Festival

Puck Fair — Origins

One of Ireland's oldest fairs, held in Killorglin each August — its roots lie in ancient Celtic harvest festivals, making it a direct link to pre-Christian Kerry.

Cultural Heritage

Traditional Music in Kerry

Kerry has one of the strongest traditional music traditions in Ireland — the Sliabh Luachra style, sean-nós singing and session culture that has been passed down for generations.
