Wellness & Spa

World-class hotel spas and the natural wellbeing of 26,000 acres of National Park on your doorstep.

Home›Activities›Wellness & Spa

Killarney has built a strong reputation as a wellness destination — partly because it has some genuinely exceptional hotel spas, and partly because the landscape itself is among the most restorative in Ireland. The combination of luxury spa facilities and the ability to walk into ancient woodland within minutes of your hotel creates a wellness proposition that few destinations in Ireland can match.

Wellness in Killarney — at a glance

  • Hotel spas Several five-star spa facilities in and around town
  • Natural wellness 26,000 acres of National Park for walking and immersion
  • Wild swimming The Killarney lakes — cold, clean and increasingly popular
  • Festivals Wander Wild Festival in September — yoga, hiking, wellness

Hotel spas

Killarney has an unusually high concentration of luxury hotels for a town of its size — a legacy of its long history as a tourism destination. Several of these hotels have world-class spa facilities. The Killarney Park Hotel, the Europe Hotel and Resort, and the Aghadoe Heights Hotel are among those with significant spa offerings — thermal suites, treatment rooms and pool facilities that make a compelling case for a dedicated spa break.

Walking in ancient woodland in the morning, a spa treatment in the afternoon — Killarney does this combination better than almost anywhere in Ireland.

Natural wellness — the National Park

The 26,000-acre National Park on Killarney's doorstep is one of the most naturally restorative environments in Ireland. The ancient woodland, the lake shores and the mountain air provide the kind of immersive nature experience that research increasingly connects to measurable improvements in mental and physical wellbeing. Walking, cycling or simply sitting by the lake in the National Park is wellness in its most fundamental form.

Wild swimming

Wild swimming in the Killarney lakes has grown significantly in popularity in recent years. The water is exceptionally clean — the lakes are within the National Park and largely unaffected by agricultural runoff. Lough Leane has several accessible entry points near Ross Castle. The water is cold year round — typically 8–15°C — and the experience of swimming in a glacially carved lake with mountains on all sides is genuinely extraordinary.

Wander Wild Festival

Each September, the Wander Wild Festival brings a programme of outdoor wellness activities to Killarney — yoga sessions in the National Park, guided mindful walks, cold water immersion, talks and workshops. It is one of the best expressions of what makes Killarney distinctive as a wellness destination — the combination of the landscape and structured wellbeing programming.
