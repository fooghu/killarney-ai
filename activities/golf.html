Golf in Killarney

Two championship courses on the shores of Lough Leane — among the most scenic golf in Ireland.

Home›Activities›Golf

Killarney Golf and Fishing Club is one of Ireland's most celebrated golf venues — and the scenery is a large part of why. The club has two championship courses, Mahony's Point and Killeen, both set on the western shore of Lough Leane with the MacGillycuddy's Reeks rising behind. Playing golf with that backdrop — the lake silver in the morning light, the mountains beyond — is an experience that goes well beyond the game itself.

Golf in Killarney — at a glance

  • Club Killarney Golf and Fishing Club
  • Courses Mahony's Point (par 72) and Killeen (par 72)
  • Setting Western shore of Lough Leane
  • Distance from town 3km from Killarney town centre
  • Visitors Welcome — book tee times in advance

Mahony's Point

Mahony's Point is the more celebrated of the two courses — its 18th hole, played alongside the lake with the mountains as a backdrop, is one of the most photographed holes in Irish golf. The course is a mix of parkland and links-influenced design, using the natural contours of the lakeshore terrain. It has hosted the Irish Open and several other major professional tournaments over the decades.

Killeen Course

The Killeen course is slightly longer and considered the more challenging of the two, with tighter fairways and more demanding approach shots. Like Mahony's Point it makes extensive use of the lakeside setting — several holes play directly alongside Lough Leane. It has also hosted professional tournament golf and is a worthy challenge for low-handicap visitors.

Playing the 18th at Mahony's Point — alongside Lough Leane with the Reeks behind — is one of those golf moments that stays with you.

Visiting golfers

Killarney Golf and Fishing Club welcomes visiting golfers throughout the year. Tee times for both courses can be booked online or by phone. Green fees vary by season — summer weekends are premium, midweek and off-season rates are more accessible. The clubhouse has a bar and restaurant and is a worthwhile stop even for non-golfers visiting the lakeside area.

Practical information

Location

Mahony's Point, Killarney — 3km from the town centre on the Killorglin road

Booking

Tee times bookable online at killarney-golf.com — book well in advance for summer weekends

Green Fees

Vary by season and day — check current rates on the club website

Equipment Hire

Club hire and trolleys available at the pro shop — no need to travel with your own clubs
