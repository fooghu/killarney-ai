Hiking & Walking

From 30-minute waterfall walks to Ireland's highest summit — Killarney has trails for every level.

Home›Activities›Hiking & Walking

Killarney is one of the great walking destinations in Ireland. The National Park, the MacGillycuddy's Reeks and the surrounding landscape offer everything from gentle lakeside strolls to serious mountain days — and the quality of the scenery at every level is exceptional. You don't need to be a mountaineer to have a remarkable walking experience here; some of the most rewarding walks in the park take less than an hour.

Walking in Killarney — at a glance

  • National Park 26,000 acres of waymarked trails — free, open year round
  • Highest point Carrauntoohil, 1,038m — Ireland's highest peak
  • Easiest walk Torc Waterfall — 30 minutes, suitable for all
  • Best full day Gap of Dunloe + lake boat return
  • Maps OSi Discovery Series Sheet 78 covers the area

Short walks — under 2 hours

Torc Waterfall

⏱ 30 min📏 1.5km🟢 Easy

The classic short walk — well-surfaced path through ancient woodland to a 20-metre waterfall. Continue above the falls for panoramic lake views most visitors miss.

Muckross Lake Loop

⏱ 2 hr📏 9km🟢 Easy

A flat loop around Muckross Lake through National Park woodland — passing Muckross Abbey, Reenadinna Yew Wood and the Meeting of the Waters.

Ross Castle to Muckross

⏱ 1.5 hr📏 6km one way🟢 Easy

Lakeside path from Ross Castle south through the National Park to Muckross House — one of the most beautiful flat walks in Kerry.

Knockreer Demesne

⏱ 1 hr📏 4km🟢 Easy

Parkland walk on the edge of Killarney town — views over Lough Leane with Ross Castle and the mountains behind. Accessible directly from the town centre.

Half-day walks

Old Kenmare Road

⏱ 3–4 hr📏 12km loop🟡 Moderate

A historic mountain route between Killarney and Kenmare — outstanding views over the Upper Lake, genuine wilderness feel, and largely off the tourist trail.

Mangerton Mountain

⏱ 4–5 hr📏 10km🟡 Moderate

A rounded mountain south of the Reeks with a high corrie lake — the Devil's Punchbowl — near the summit. Less dramatic than the Reeks but more accessible.

Full-day mountain walks

Carrauntoohil via Devil's Ladder

⏱ 5–6 hr📏 12km🔴 Strenuous

Ireland's highest peak via the standard route — a serious mountain day requiring proper equipment and navigation ability. Not for beginners.

Gap of Dunloe + Lake Return

⏱ Full day📏 11km + boat🟡 Moderate

The classic Killarney full day — walk the Gap, take a boat back across the three lakes. One of the great day experiences in Ireland.

Purple Mountain

⏱ 4–5 hr📏 10km🟡 Moderate

Excellent mountain views over the Gap of Dunloe without the challenge of Carrauntoohil — the ideal step up for confident walkers.

Coomloughra Horseshoe

⏱ 7–9 hr📏 16km🔴 Challenging

Ireland's finest ridge walk — Carrauntoohil, Beenkeragh and Caher in a single day. Experienced hillwalkers only.

💡

Local tip: The National Park trails around Muckross and Ross Castle are excellent in any weather — well-surfaced and sheltered by woodland. Save the mountain walks for clear days and always check the Met Éireann mountain forecast before heading above 600 metres.

Practical information

Maps

OSi Discovery Series Sheet 78 covers Killarney and the Reeks — essential for any mountain walk. Available in Killarney town

Equipment

Waterproof jacket and trousers, sturdy boots and a map are essential for any walk above the valley floor in Kerry

Guided Walks

Several Killarney-based companies offer guided walks at all levels — recommended for Carrauntoohil and the Reeks

National Park Trails

Free, waymarked and open year round — maps available at the National Park visitor centre at Muckross
