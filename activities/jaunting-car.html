Jaunting Cars

A horse-drawn tradition as old as Killarney tourism itself — the jaunting car with a local jarvey.

Home›Activities›Jaunting Cars

The jaunting car is as Killarney as the lakes and the mountains. A traditional Irish horse-drawn vehicle — an open-sided trap with passengers seated sideways — driven by a jarvey who narrates the journey with local stories, history and a healthy dose of Kerry wit. It is not a gimmick or a theme park attraction; it is a genuine local tradition that has been running in Killarney for over two centuries and remains one of the most pleasant ways to explore the National Park.

Jaunting Cars — at a glance

  • Vehicle Traditional Irish jaunting car — open-sided horse-drawn trap
  • Driver Called a jarvey — usually a local with extensive knowledge
  • Departs from Kenmare Place (town centre) or Muckross House
  • Routes National Park, Gap of Dunloe, lake circuit
  • Duration 30 minutes to full day depending on route

The tradition

Jaunting cars have been transporting visitors around Killarney since the early 19th century, when the town first became a popular destination for aristocratic tourists doing the Grand Tour of Ireland. The jarveys — the drivers — developed a reputation for colourful local knowledge, storytelling and a sharp eye for the passing scenery. That tradition continues today; a good jarvey is as much guide and entertainer as driver.

A jarvey on the Killarney lakes road in the early morning — the horse's hooves on the tarmac, the mountains reflected in the water — is one of those Kerry moments that stays with you.

Routes

The most popular jaunting car routes cover the National Park between Killarney town and Muckross House, and the Gap of Dunloe from Kate Kearney's Cottage. The Gap of Dunloe by jaunting car — combined with a boat return across the lakes — is the classic full-day Killarney experience that has been running for over 150 years.

Town to Muckross

⏱ 1–1.5 hr🟢 Easy

From Kenmare Place through the National Park to Muckross House and back — the standard tour, passing Ross Castle and the lake shore.

Gap of Dunloe

⏱ 2–3 hr🟡 Moderate

From Kate Kearney's Cottage through the mountain pass — the most dramatic jaunting car route. Often combined with a boat return across the lakes.

💡

Local tip: Always agree the fare before you set off — prices are per person and vary by route. Tipping the jarvey is customary and appreciated. Morning departures from Kenmare Place are quieter than the midday rush.

Practical information

Departure Points

Kenmare Place in Killarney town centre and Muckross House car park — jarveys available throughout the day in season

Pricing

Priced per person — agree the fare before departing. Typical tours range from €15–€25 per person

Season

Available year round but most active April to October. Quieter in winter but still operating

Families

Excellent for families with young children — an engaging, relaxed way to see the National Park without walking
