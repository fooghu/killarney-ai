Kerry produces it, Killarney serves it

Kerry lamb, Atlantic seafood, artisan cheeses and craft beers — Killarney sits in the middle of one of Ireland's finest food-producing counties. The town has a dining scene that punches well above its size, from tucked-away laneways to lively pubs with traditional sessions running every night.
