These are events that happen every year without fail — some for centuries, some more recent but already embedded in the calendar. All of them are reasons to visit Killarney at a particular time of year.

Ancient Celtic Tradition · 3 Days

Puck Fair, Killorglin

One of Ireland's oldest and most unusual festivals — a wild goat is crowned King Puck for three days in Killorglin, 20 minutes from Killarney. Music, markets and ancient tradition since the 17th century.
