St Patrick's Day

Killarney celebrates Ireland's national day with a town parade, live music and a festive atmosphere on 17th March.

Home›Events›St Patrick's Day

St Patrick's Day in Killarney is a proper national holiday celebration — a town parade through the main street, music in the pubs from early in the day, and the particular festive atmosphere of a town that knows how to welcome visitors without losing its own character. The 17th of March brings locals and visitors together in a way that feels genuinely inclusive rather than performative.

St Patrick's Day — at a glance

  • Date 17th March — every year
  • Main event Town parade through Killarney town centre
  • Music Live music and traditional sessions across the pubs all day
  • Character Family-friendly during the day, livelier in the evening
  • Entry Free — street event and parade

The parade

The Killarney St Patrick's Day parade takes place through the town centre — typically on the morning of the 17th, occasionally moved to the nearest Sunday if the 17th falls mid-week. The parade features floats from local businesses and community groups, marching bands, Irish dancers and the general organised chaos that characterises Irish small-town parades at their best. The route through the main street draws large crowds and the atmosphere is warm and family-oriented.

St Patrick's Day in a Kerry town — the parade, the music, the green jerseys — is one of those Irish experiences that is genuinely what it appears to be.

Music and pubs

The pubs in Killarney open early on St Patrick's Day and the music runs from midday onwards. Traditional sessions, live bands and the general social energy of a national holiday make the town centre lively throughout the day and into the evening. The pubs along the main street and the lanes off it are all busy — if you want a seat for the evening, arrive early.

Practical information

Date

17th March every year — parade time typically late morning, check local announcements closer to the date

Accommodation

St Patrick's weekend is busy — book accommodation in advance, particularly if the 17th falls on a Friday or Saturday

Families

The parade and daytime events are very family-friendly — the evening pub scene is livelier and less suitable for young children

Getting There

Killarney is well-served by Bus Éireann and train from Cork, Limerick and Dublin — a good option for avoiding parking on a busy day
