Killarney Junction Festival

Live music across Killarney venues every autumn — national and international acts in an intimate setting.

Home›Events›Killarney Junction Festival

The Killarney Junction Festival has established itself as a welcome addition to the autumn cultural calendar — a multi-day music festival bringing a programme of national and international acts to venues across Killarney town each October. The festival takes advantage of the town's strong live music infrastructure and its off-season availability to create something with a more intimate, music-focused character than the large summer events.

Killarney Junction Festival — at a glance

  • When October — exact dates vary year to year
  • Location Multiple venues across Killarney town
  • Music National and international acts — varied genres
  • Character Intimate venue festival — closer to the music than large outdoor events
  • Tickets Individual events and festival passes available

The festival

The Junction Festival uses Killarney's existing venue network — pubs, hotels, the INEC arena and smaller intimate spaces — to create a town-wide music experience across several days. The programming has typically leaned towards folk, roots, singer-songwriter and traditional music with some crossover acts, reflecting both the town's musical culture and the tastes of the autumn audience. The indoor, venue-based format gives the festival a different feel from the large summer outdoor events — closer to the music, more conversational, better suited to the autumn weather.

October in Killarney with live music across the town — the leaves turning in the National Park and good music every night. It's a combination that works.

Why October works

October is one of the most beautiful months in Killarney — the National Park is in full autumn colour, the red deer rut is at its peak, and the town has breathing room after the intensity of the summer season. The Junction Festival has recognised this and built an event that leans into the season rather than fighting it. Visitors who come for the music often find they've also arrived at one of the best times of year to experience the wider Killarney landscape.

Practical information

Dates

October annually — check killarneyjunctionfestival.com for exact dates and programme each year

Tickets

Individual event tickets and festival passes available online — popular acts sell out in advance

Accommodation

October is off-peak — accommodation more available and better value than summer, but book ahead once dates are confirmed

Venues

Multiple venues across Killarney — INEC, hotel ballrooms and pub stages. Check the programme for specific venue information
