About Killarney

A town built around natural wonder

Killarney sits at the edge of Ireland's oldest and largest national park — 26,000 acres of ancient oak woodland, blanket bog, mountain peaks and the famous Lakes of Killarney. It's been drawing visitors for over two centuries, and the reasons haven't changed.

MacGillycuddy's Reeks, Ireland's highest mountain range, rises to the south-west. The Gap of Dunloe cuts through them like a wound in the earth. Muckross House sits quietly on the lakeshore, unchanged since Victorian times. The pubs fill with traditional music every night of the week.

Killarney.ai is a guide built by someone who lives here — the trails worth walking, the sessions worth sitting through, the history worth knowing and the spots that don't make it into brochures.
